# Faculty mapping (South African university faculties)

Assign an opportunity to every faculty whose graduates it would genuinely accept. Go by the degrees the ad asks for first, then by the work itself.

| Key | Faculty | Typical degrees / fields | Typical opportunities |
|---|---|---|---|
| EMS | Economic & Management Sciences (Commerce) | BCom (Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Management, Marketing, HR, Supply Chain/Logistics, Information Systems/Informatics), BAcc, CA stream, Actuarial (BCom route), Public Admin, Tourism Mgmt | Audit/SAICA trainee, banking, consulting, sales/marketing, HR, procurement, business analyst |
| SCIENCE | Natural Sciences | BSc Computer Science, IT, Maths, Applied Maths, Statistics, Data Science, Actuarial (BSc route), Physics, Chemistry, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Biology, Geology, Environmental Science, GIS | Software dev, data/analytics, quant, lab/research, mining geology, pharma QA |
| ENGINEERING | Engineering & the Built Environment | BEng/BSc Eng (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronic, Chemical, Industrial, Mining, Metallurgical, Computer), BTech/Diploma Eng, Architecture, Quantity Surveying, Construction Mgmt, Town Planning | EIT programmes, plant/operations, infrastructure, mining, construction |
| SOCIAL SCIENCES | Humanities & Social Sciences | BA/BSocSci: Psychology, Sociology, Political Science, International Relations, Communication/Media/Journalism, Languages, Development Studies, Social Work, Industrial Psychology (often also EMS) | Comms/PR, research, policy, HR/talent, NGO/development, social work |
| AGRISCIENCE | Agricultural Sciences | BSc Agric, Agricultural Economics (also EMS), Animal/Plant/Soil Science, Food Science, Viticulture/Oenology, Forestry, Conservation Ecology | Agribusiness, food production, farming co-ops, agri-finance |
| LAW | Law | LLB, BA/BCom Law | Candidate attorney / articles, legal intern, compliance, contracts, company secretarial |
| EDUCATION | Education | BEd, PGCE, any degree + teaching qualification | Teaching posts, tutoring, ed-tech, curriculum, learning & development |

## Rules of thumb

- **"Any degree" / "any field"** graduate programmes (jobmailer, strict): analytical / quantitative ones go to SCIENCE, ENGINEERING and LAW with wording like "Any quantitative / analytical degree". Do not add EMS, SOCIAL SCIENCES or EDUCATION just because the ad is open.
- **Information Systems / Informatics** sits in EMS at most SA universities, but IT-support and data-processing roles go in SCIENCE only. Use EMS only when the work is commerce (reporting, analytics for finance, business analysis).
- **Actuarial** → EMS and SCIENCE.
- **Industrial Psychology / HR** → EMS and SOCIAL SCIENCE.
- **SAICA / CA(SA) training programmes (trainee accountant, articles)** → degree is "Bachelor of Accounting / BCom Accounting Sciences (SAICA-accredited)" — not general BCom. EMS only. (User rule, 2026-10-14.)
- **Agricultural Economics** → AGRISCIENCE and EMS.
- **Compliance / risk** roles that accept LLB or BCom → LAW and EMS.
- **Data analytics** accepting Stats, Maths, CS, Economics → SCIENCE and EMS (and ENGINEERING if it says "engineering" too).
- When the degree is inferred, classify by the inferred degree but don't over-spread — a role must plausibly hire that faculty's graduates.
- A user note in the queue ("this one is for law students") overrides your guess.

## Jobmailer additions (user rules)

- A faculty needs a named degree in the ad or clearly belonging work. A corporate graduate programme is not EMS by default.
- Each faculty gets its own short degree wording for the same job (BCom ... under EMS, BSc ... under Science, LLB ... under Law).
- SOCIAL SCIENCES: Media / Communication / Psychology / Sociology / Political Science / Languages named in the ad.
- EDUCATION: only when the ad names BEd / PGCE / teaching. Foundation Phase = Gr R–3, Intermediate Phase = Gr 4–6.
- AGRISCIENCE: skip the faculty when nothing in the shared jobs names an agricultural degree.
